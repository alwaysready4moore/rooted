# Rooted — public design decisions

Updated 2026-10-20. This file intentionally omits unreleased story secrets and character histories.

| Topic | Status | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Genre | Approved | Cozy fantasy community life sim, based on reciprocity |
| Exploration | Approved | Fully walkable top-down overworld |
| Engine | Provisional | Godot 4 with GDScript; runtime validation pending |
| Rowan's farm | Approved | Cultivated plants only — crops, herbs, seasonings, flowers and fruit |
| Animal husbandry | Approved | No livestock/ranching at Rowan's farm; another Hearthshore farmer cares for animals and supplies related goods |
| Pets | Approved direction | Optional companion pets, not livestock producers; mechanics pending |
| Starting duties | Approved | Art deliberately starts Rowan with manageable plant care rather than responsibility for animals |
| Cooking | Approved direction | Experiment without knowing a recipe; residents teach preparation tips and improvements |
| Living town | Goal, unbuilt | NPC routines, visits, moves, school travel, evolving businesses |
| Lux's Little Tea Shop | Approved | Independent small game to evaluate Godot, art and gameplay before scaling to Rooted |

The full, living Rooted Game Bible, GDD, cast, crop workbook and art references remain in Google Drive. Do not mirror unreleased story material to this public repository.
