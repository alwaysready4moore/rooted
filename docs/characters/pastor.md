# The Pastor

## Character purpose

The pastor is a practical human community liaison whose influence comes from work, judgment, and trust rather than magic. They connect humans, magical people, and different species without pretending those groups are interchangeable.

They should feel like a person with a vocation, not a generic source of serene advice. Their daily life may include food distribution, visits, repairs, funerals, weddings, mediation, accessibility arrangements, and explanations of misunderstood customs.

## Confirmed canon

- The pastor is an ordinary human, not a mage or secretly chosen figure.
- They liaise among humans, magical people, and different species.
- Their faith centers nature, mutual care, and people's relationship to the Flow.
- They do practical community work as well as lead gatherings.
- They do not know Art is a reaper.
- Art attends sincerely and enthusiastically.
- The pastor's marriageability has not been decided.

## Tentative characterization

The strongest current version is **Pastor Ellis Reed**: grounded, observant, warm without being sugary, dryly funny, and a little tired. Ellis can be stubborn, curt when challenged, and prone to treating being needed as proof of usefulness.

Ellis respects magic without romanticizing it. Power does not confer wisdom, kindness, or spiritual maturity. A possible central tension is that Ellis believes nobody should endure suffering alone while hiding their own strain.

## Tentative faith

The Flow belongs to birth, death, weather, decay, growth, magic, and change. Ellis does not teach that it rewards virtue, guarantees fairness, notices prayer, or gives tragedy a reason. Meaning and obligation arise from how people respond to one another.

> The Flow does not promise fairness. People do.

This is a working summary, not locked dialogue. Ellis distinguishes doctrine, inherited story, local custom, metaphor, and guesswork. Art respects that humility.

## Community role

Possible responsibilities include maintaining a neutral meeting hall, coordinating aid, mediating disputes, arranging cross-species accommodations, officiating rites, visiting residents, translating customs, and doing unglamorous maintenance. These duties let the pastor know much of the town without becoming omniscient.

## Relationships

Ellis does not treat Rowan as a project or demand confession, belief, recovery, or disclosure. They offer low-pressure, concrete ways to participate. The relationship should include misreadings and irritation as well as trust; Ellis can offer the wrong task or mistake withdrawal for a wish to be left alone.

Art is Ellis's most perplexing congregant. She asks sincere questions whose implications sound alarming. Art respects Ellis because they do not claim authority they lack. Ellis does not know her questions sometimes come from direct knowledge.

## Marriageability question

### Current lean

The current preference is **probably not marriageable**, but this is not canon. A non-romantic route could become one of the game's deepest relationships and challenge the assumption that every appealing adult is a possible spouse.

One possibility is that Ellis has had romantic relationships but does not want to organize their life around partnership. Other possibilities—personal celibacy, an existing relationship, or a romance route—remain open.

### Design test

- Does romance reveal something unavailable to a profound friendship?
- Would marriage reduce Ellis to a reward structure?
- Can a non-romantic route receive equal depth and recognition?
- If romanceable, how does the route avoid treating professional care as flirtation?
- If not, how early and honestly does the game communicate that boundary?

Until answered, no scene should depend on Ellis being romantically available or unavailable.

## Contradictions to avoid

- Do not make the pastor secretly magical or automatically correct about every culture.
- Do not make faith a reliable technical explanation of the Flow.
- Do not turn practical service into flawless self-sacrifice.
- Do not let emotional intimacy silently become romantic availability.
- Do not imply Art attends only as a joke; her interest and respect are sincere.

## Unresolved questions

- Is Ellis Reed the final name?
- What are the pastor's age, gender, pronouns, appearance, and voice?
- What tradition and history surround their faith?
- Where does their authority end, and who holds them accountable?
- What past mistake damaged trust?
- What do they want outside their vocation?
- What is their relationship to family, romance, celibacy, and marriage?
- Are they marriageable?
- What first-season conflict reveals both their usefulness and their limits?
