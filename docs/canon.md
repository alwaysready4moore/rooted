# Canon Ledger

This ledger separates established facts from active possibilities. Appealing ideas are not canon until deliberately confirmed.

## Confirmed canon

### Premise

- Rooted is a story-first cozy fantasy life sim about belonging rather than saving the world.
- Its small community includes humans, magical people, and multiple species.
- It has continuing seasons and no true ending.
- Art's bar is the primary social hub and the town's living room.
- Physical projects matter because they create or restore social connections.
- No game engine has been chosen.

### Rowan and Art

- Rowan is fully human, has no capacity for magic, and is not secretly chosen.
- Rowan is on the brink of a nervous breakdown and suicide before arriving.
- Art is secretly a high-ranking reaper assigned to wait for Rowan's death.
- Art intervenes, claims a vague connection to Rowan's parent or aunt, and offers work at her bar plus a room upstairs.
- The job is real, and its ordinary tasks give Rowan manageable next steps.
- Art does not save Rowan to use them, recruit them, earn gratitude, or collect repayment.
- Art never pressures Rowan to stay.
- Art is known for taking in temporary workers and people who need somewhere to land, camouflaging Rowan's arrival.
- The community does not know Art's nature or the truth of Rowan's rescue.
- Rowan may never learn the complete truth.

### Mystery

- Art's secret appears through hints, inconsistencies, rare conditions, records, and reactions rather than exposition.
- Individual clues remain dismissible; accumulation gives them weight.
- Art never winks at the audience or speaks only to validate a theory.
- The game need not confirm the player's interpretation or reward discovery.

### Pastor

- The pastor is an ordinary human, not a mage.
- They are a practical liaison among humans, magical people, and different species.
- Their faith concerns nature, mutual care, and people's relationship to the Flow.
- They perform tangible community service as well as religious work.
- They do not know Art is a reaper.
- Art attends sincerely, fascinated by beliefs about the morally neutral Flow.
- The pastor's marriageability is unresolved.

## Tentative ideas

- Belonging may be represented by hidden “roots” formed through reliable participation.
- Rowan may gradually help run the bar without a formal promotion.
- Relationships may separately track familiarity, trust, affection, respect, strain, and knowledge.
- World state may distinguish objective events, individual memories, collective records, and player knowledge.
- The Flow may affect growth, weather, magic, decay, death, or memory without acting as a moral force.
- Art's clues may include knowledge Rowan never shared, inconsistent family stories, an advance ticket, an assignment record, or another reaper's remark.
- The pastor's working name is **Pastor Ellis Reed**.
- Ellis may be practical, warm but dry, mildly tired, stubborn, and poor at accepting care.
- A deep, explicitly non-romantic route is the leading possibility.

## Contradictions and superseded ideas

### Why Art chose Rowan

An early idea made Rowan a useful non-magical outsider recruited to investigate the Flow. Later development established that Art was assigned to Rowan's death and intervened out of compassion.

**Ruling:** the rescue motive is canon. If Rowan later matters to a larger conflict, that importance grows from the life and relationships they build afterward.

### Ending versus continuation

An early proposal imagined a final shot revealing part of Art's assignment record. Later discussion rejected a true ending for a farming life sim.

**Ruling:** Rooted continues across seasons. A record may appear as an optional late event, but not as a definitive ending or complete explanation.

### Art's other interventions

One idea has Art repeatedly breaking rules across decades. Another makes Rowan the sole assigned death she directly prevents while allowing that she has helped others.

**Ruling:** unresolved. Do not imply a pattern of prevented assigned deaths yet.

## Unresolved questions

- What are the town's name and daily geography?
- What practical arc shapes Rowan's first season?
- How much contact does Rowan retain with family?
- What is the Flow, and what remains unknowable about it?
- What rules govern reapers, rank, assignments, and intervention?
- Does Art face consequences for saving Rowan?
- How much can Rowan suspect, and which clues vary with player choices?
- How is Rowan's mental-health history represented carefully in ordinary play?
- Which characters beyond Rowan, Art, and the pastor are confirmed canon?
- Which relationships can become romantic?
- Is the pastor marriageable?
- What are the pastor's final name, identity, history, and personal relationship to romance?
- What is the smallest playable slice that demonstrates belonging through routine?
- How central are bar work, gardening, crafting, cooking, and other cozy activities?
- What accessibility and content-warning support should accompany the suicide-related foundation?
